**Why the smaller SGS-only alternative is not selected:** `SubgridStresses` executes `ComputeViscsWM` before SGS, then `ComputeUVWFluxesWM`, `BoundViscosity`, immersed-boundary corrections and possible scalar-diffusivity work afterwards ([dynamics.f90:869–908](../../results/checked-plan-application-20261014/seed/ELMM/src/dynamics.f90)). `ComputeUVWFluxesWM` contains 18 wall-list calls, original parallel loops, pointer association and metadata updates ([wallmodels.f90:2159](../../results/checked-plan-application-20261014/seed/ELMM/src/wallmodels.f90)); viscosity boundaries are separate native work ([scalarboundaries.f90:326](../../results/checked-plan-application-20261014/seed/ELMM/src/scalarboundaries.f90)). This can require substantial proof and synchronization work even when host velocity mirrors are current. A window ending there has no downstream GPU viscosity consumer and must publish viscosity on exit. Extending its lifetime alone has no established bulk-transfer saving over SGS direct. Reaching diffusion from SGS would reintroduce the advection, filtering and force interval.

**Transfer bounds are conditional, not predictions.** Let `M` be the bytes of momentum sections passed from diffusion to accumulation, `N` the viscosity bytes uploaded per separately offloaded component, `P` the native-correction/cleanup traffic added by residency, and `V` a repeated velocity upload. For the same section sets and three independently offloaded component kernels, the identifiable removable edges obey `saving ≤ M + 2N − P`. The `2N` term is zero when the comparison already shares viscosity. Final momentum publication is still required by the next stage, so a complete momentum round trip cannot be counted as removed.

On the current grids, momentum interiors total **11.9375 MiB**, full velocity allocations **14.9568 MiB**, and full viscosity storage **4.6567 MiB**. Thus the example ceiling for those particular edges is **21.2510 MiB per RK stage** before native traffic, assuming an interior momentum upload and two full redundant viscosity uploads. It is not a bound on all application traffic or an observed saving. A larger proved SGS→diffusion interval could additionally target a repeated velocity upload and viscosity round trip, at most `V + 2N` for those named edges before native traffic; that interval is not admitted.

Against the actual SGS-direct control with native Convection, the suffix introduces GPU inputs and output publication that the control does not transfer. A simple one-upload/one-publication accounting is approximately ten scalar-field uploads and six scalar-field downloads per stage before halos and wall corrections. Consequently the **net saving versus SGS direct is unknown and may be negative**. The next decision must use the observed full ledger; these symbolic ceilings cannot justify performance claims.
